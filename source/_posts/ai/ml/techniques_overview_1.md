In this guide, we will review popular machine learning techniques with a brief overview of their applications, complexity levels, and some historical context. This list provides a starting point for understanding core machine learning methods and their practical uses.

---
<a href="" target="_blank"></a>

## 1. **Linear Regression**
   - **Overview**: A fundamental statistical method used to predict a dependent variable based on an independent variable. Commonly applied in forecasting and trend analysis.
   - **Complexity**: ⭐
   - **Inventor**: Sir Francis Galton (DOB: 1822-1911) and Karl Pearson (DOB: 1857-1936)
   - **Key Publication**: 
     - Book:<a href="https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/pdf/10.1098/rsta.1896.0007" target="_blank">Pearson, K. (1896). "Mathematical Contributions to the Theory of Evolution</a>["](../../../../../docs/other/pearson-henrici-1997-vii-mathematical-contributions-to-the-theory-of-evolution-iii-regression-heredity-and-panmixia.pdf)

## 2. **Logistic Regression**
   - **Overview**: Primarily used for binary classification, logistic regression estimates the probability of an outcome and is used widely in classification tasks like spam detection.
   - **Complexity**: ⭐⭐
   - **Inventor**: David Cox (DOB: 1924-2022)
   - **Key Publication**: <a href="https://sci-hub.se/10.1037/h0042519" target="_blank">Cox, D.R. (1958). "The Regression Analysis of Binary Sequences."</a>

## 3. **Decision Trees**
   - **Overview**: A tree-like structure for decision-making and predictive modeling. Suitable for classification and regression problems.
   - **Complexity**: ⭐⭐
   - **Inventor**: Ross Quinlan (DOB: 1944)
   - **Key Publication**: <a href="../../../../../docs/other/quinlan_j.r._1986_induction_of_decision_trees.pdf" target="_blank">Quinlan, J.R. (1986). "Induction of Decision Trees."</a>


## 4. **Random Forests**
   - **Overview**: An ensemble method that builds multiple decision trees and combines them to improve accuracy. Known for its robustness and ease of use.
   - **Complexity**: ⭐⭐⭐
   - **Inventor**: Leo Breiman (DOB: 1928-2005)
   - **Key Publication**: <a href="https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~breiman/randomforest2001.pdf" target="_blank">Breiman, L. (2001). "Random Forests."</a>


## 5. **Support Vector Machines (SVM)**
   - **Overview**: An effective classification technique that finds the hyperplane maximizing the margin between data classes. Widely used in image and text classification.
   - **Complexity**: ⭐⭐⭐
   - **Inventor**: Vladimir Vapnik (DOB: 1936)
   - **Key Publication**: Book: <a href="../../../../../docs/other/vladimir-vapnik-the-nature-of-statistical-learning-springer-2010.pdf" target="_blank">Vapnik, V. (1995). "The Nature of Statistical Learning Theory."</a>

## 6. **K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)**
   - **Overview**: A simple, non-parametric method used for classification and regression. It predicts outcomes based on the 'k' nearest data points.
   - **Complexity**: ⭐⭐
   - **Inventor**: Evelyn Fix (DOB: 1904-1987) and Joseph Hodges (DOB: 1922-2000)
   - **Key Publication**: 
     - Paper: 
       - DOI: 10.2307/1403797
       - <a href="https://sci-hub.se/10.2307/1403797" target="_blank">Fix, E., & Hodges, J.L. (1951). "Discriminatory Analysis."</a>
     - Book: <a href="https://books.google.com.ua/books?hl=en&lr=&id=s85PAQAAMAAJ&oi=fnd&pg=PP1&dq=DOI+Fix,+E.,+%26+Hodges,+J.L.+(1951).+%E2%80%9CDiscriminatory+Analysis.%E2%80%9D&ots=MKP8x31F3a&sig=YAfjZLsq1f02Wby3vzoQAI00WkM&redir_esc=y#v=onepage&q&f=false" target="_blank">Fix, E., & Hodges, J.L. (1951). "Discriminatory Analysis."</a>

## 7. **Naive Bayes**
   - **Overview**: Based on Bayes' theorem, this technique is widely used for text classification due to its simplicity and effectiveness with large datasets.
   - **Complexity**: ⭐
   - **Inventor**: Thomas Bayes (DOB: 1701-1761)
   - **Key Publication**: 
     - Paper: <a href="../../../../../docs/other/Bayes_T_1763_An_Essay_towards_solving_a_Problem_in_the_Doctrine_of_Chances.pdf" target="_blank">Bayes, T. (1763). "An Essay towards solving a Problem in the Doctrine of Chances."</a>

## 8. **Gradient Boosting**
   - **Overview**: An ensemble technique that combines weak learners to minimize errors iteratively. Known for its high accuracy in structured data tasks.
   - **Complexity**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
   - **Inventor**: Jerome Friedman (DOB: 1939)
   - **Key Publication**: 
     - DOI: 10.2307/2699986 
     - Paper: <a href="https://sci-hub.se/10.2307/2699986" target="_blank">Friedman, J.H. (2001). "Greedy Function Approximation: A Gradient Boosting Machine."</a>

## 9. **Neural Networks**
   - **Overview**: Inspired by biological neural networks, these algorithms are used in complex tasks like image recognition and language processing.
   - **Complexity**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
   - **Inventor**: Frank Rosenblatt (DOB: 1928-1971)
   - **Key Publication**:
     - Paper: <a href="https://www.ling.upenn.edu/courses/cogs501/Rosenblatt1958.pdf" target="_blank">Rosenblatt, F. (1958). "The Perceptron: A Probabilistic Model for Information Storage and Organization."</a>

## 10. **K-Means Clustering**
   - **Overview**: An unsupervised algorithm that partitions data into 'k' clusters based on similarity. Commonly used for market segmentation and image compression.
   - **Complexity**: ⭐⭐
   - **Inventor**: Stuart Lloyd (DOB: 1933-2006)
   - **Key Publication**: 
     - Paper: 
       - DOI:10.1109/TIT.1982.1056489
       - <a href="https://sci-hub.se/10.1109/TIT.1982.1056489" target="_blank">Lloyd, S.P. (1982). "Least Squares Quantization in PCM."</a>

## 11. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
   - **Overview**: A dimensionality reduction technique that transforms data into a set of orthogonal components, enhancing interpretability without sacrificing information.
   - **Complexity**: ⭐⭐
   - **Inventor**: Karl Pearson (DOB: 1857-1936)
   - **Key Publication**: 
     - Paper: 
       - DOI:10.1080/14786440109462720
       - <a href="https://sci-hub.se/10.1080/14786440109462720" target="_blank">Pearson, K. (1901). "On Lines and Planes of Closest Fit to Systems of Points."</a>

## 12. **Reinforcement Learning (RL)**
   - **Overview**: An area focused on training models to make sequences of decisions, particularly for game playing and robotics.
   - **Complexity**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
   - **Inventor**: Richard Sutton (DOB: 1952)
   - **Key Publication**: 
     - Books in Brief:
         - DOI:10.1109/TNN.1998.712192
         - <a href="https://sci-hub.se/10.1109/TNN.1998.712192" target="_blank">Sutton, R., & Barto, A. (1998). "Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction."</a>
     -  <a href="https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Reinforcement-Learning%3A-An-Introduction-Sutton-Barto/97efafdb4a3942ab3efba53ded7413199f79c054" target="_blank">semanticscholar link</a>
     -  <a href="http://incompleteideas.net/book/RLbook2020.pdf" target="_blank">Book: Sutton, R., & Barto, A. (1998). "Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction." - online_1</a>
     -  <a href="../../../../../docs/other/sutton_r_and_barto_a_1998_reinforcement_learning_an_introduction.pdf" target="_blank">Book: Sutton, R., & Barto, A. (1998). "Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction." - online_2</a>
  ---

These techniques represent a wide spectrum of machine learning methods used across industries. Whether for predictive modeling, classification, clustering, or decision-making, understanding these core methods is foundational to exploring more advanced machine learning concepts.
